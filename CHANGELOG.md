## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Party quiz «Трансформация Безработного» about Phil: 10 random questions per game, 4 answers each, in Russian.
- Shared leaderboard with attempts, total points and average score for every player.
- Submit a fact about Phil and Root AI - model b1t quiz v1.1 turns it into new funny questions that join the game right away.
- Free-throw second chance: after a wrong answer, make a basketball free throw to try the question again.
- Achievements and secret easter eggs that follow you across devices.
- 3D cartoon Phil who walks along the screen, reacts to the game, comments, takes smoke breaks and shoots at the hoop.
- Side-court basketball: drag and throw the ball at a hoop beside the quiz, with your own basket counter.
- Three full-screen mini-games: a cigarette-break clicker, a Divine Orb farming league and a bath survival game.
- Phil's shared talent tree: everything players do levels him up and unlocks new animations and sharper lines.
- Live home-page feed of what everyone is doing on the site, with filters and paging.
- RMT exchange page with the cheapest Divine Orb prices on FunPay for Path of Exile 1 and 2, charts, an order book and season timelines.
- Savings goal with a season-aware price forecast that shows how long farming would take to afford a new PC.
- Invite-only portal builder: members create their own quiz portal about themselves or a friend, filled in by Root AI - model b1t portal v1.0 from photos and facts.
- Admin page to review, edit, regenerate and delete player-made questions and feed events.
